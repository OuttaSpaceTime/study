## Session 1 — Change (07:12)
- **Files:** `scripts/srs_pressure.py`, `tests/test_srs_pressure.py`
- **Change:** Reformatted `scripts/srs-pressure --human` deck breakdown. Old format (`Software Engineering — 32 due (17 new, 0 learning, 251 review)`) mixed two axes on one line — `Due` is a status flag while `New/Learning/Review` are mutually-exclusive states — and silently dropped relearning/suspended cards so totals didn't reconcile. New format prints total cards, then a `State buckets:` line that includes an `N other (relearning/suspended)` segment when needed (so `new+learning+review+other = total`), then a separate `N due now (cuts across state buckets above)` line. Updated the existing test for the new format and added a second test covering the case where state sum equals total (no `other` segment).
- **Why:** Developer ran `scripts/srs-pressure --human` and called out the confusion: 17+0+251 ≠ 32, and the line read like a single sum. With 274 total cards in the deck, the old display also hid 6 cards in relearning/suspended state. New layout reconciles the numbers and makes the state-vs-status distinction explicit.
