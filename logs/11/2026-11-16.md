## Query 1 (07:55)
- **Question:** Are 4NF and 5NF practical for real-world projects and actually used?
- **Wiki hits:** [[sql/database-normalization]] (one line: Microsoft calls 3NF enough for most apps; 4NF and 5NF rarely considered in practice)
- **Card hits:** none
- **Web check:** refines: Date & Fagin 1992 (TODS) proves 3NF + every key simple implies 5NF, and BCNF + some key simple implies 4NF, so violations need composite (often all-key) tables; Wu 1992 found 4NF violations in over 20% of 40 real databases, so they are not that rare when tables get merged. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourth_normal_form , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fifth_normal_form
- **Source:** mixed
- **Answer:** Target 3NF/BCNF then denormalize deliberately; 4NF comes free from one junction table per many-to-many and breaks when two are merged; 5NF (cyclic join dependencies) is rare and almost never a design target; analytics uses star schemas, 6NF is niche.
