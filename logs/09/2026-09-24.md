# 2026-09-24

## Session 1 — Change (20:52)
- **Files:** scripts/ankisync/master.py, tests/test_ankisync_master.py
- **Change:** `import_card` no longer writes a literal `0.0` for stability/difficulty when Anki reports no FSRS `memory_state`; added `_fallback_memory_state()`, which derives stability from the card's real Anki interval (floored at 1 day) and a neutral difficulty, but leaves genuinely New cards at `0.0`. Added 5 regression tests. Also backfilled 120 already-corrupted cards in master.db (stability/difficulty were `0.0` despite real review history) using the same formula.
- **Why:** Discovered mid-`/study` session — a card with 7 reps and an established interval (shown as maturity "familiar") got rated Good and came back as a 10-minute intra-day repeat with FSRS's raw New-card init values (stability 3.173, difficulty 5.28243442 — exact matches to `w[2]` and `init_difficulty(Good)`). Traced this to `scheduler.ts`'s `sanitizeForReview()`, which silently resets any non-New card with `stability<=0` to a fresh New card — a safety net for exactly this kind of corruption. Root cause was `import_card`'s `sched.stability or 0.0`: any card imported from Anki whose note predates FSRS being enabled there (or hasn't been reviewed since) has `memory_state=None`, and that was silently coerced to `0.0` — indistinguishable from the corruption sentinel. Affected 123 of 344 review cards (36% of the deck) before the fix.
